# The Golden Boundary — An Invitation

A self-contained, click-through fly-through built for the Royal University of Bhutan.
No frameworks, no external requests, no dependencies — one HTML file.

**Live:** https://aukora.xyz/royal-invite/
**Companion documents:** https://github.com/aumara-xyz/golden-horizon-principle/blob/main/research/AN_INVITATION.md

Password: the shape it's about, nine letters, lowercase.

Six scenes, advanced by click, tap, or the space/enter/right-arrow keys: harp-string waves
settling toward a critical line; a tesseract forming from the settled points; a cube's twenty-seven
faces enumerated live on one shared facet; two cells meeting along that facet with the forced
orientation flip and the fixed centre; the cost theorem `H(B) ≥ I(X;Y)` as two overlapping circles;
and the closing invitation.

Every claim on the page matches the research documents exactly — nothing here overstates them.
